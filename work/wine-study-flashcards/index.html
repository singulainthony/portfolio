Wine Study Flashcards

A flashcard series on Instagram for WSET wine students. Nearly 250 numbered question cards built an audience of 4,400+ alongside the study platform at flashcards.wine.

Studying for a WSET wine exam is mostly memorizing: regions, grapes, producers, and a vocabulary in four or five languages. I was a wine student doing exactly that, so I started posting a running series of question cards on Instagram to help other WSET students study.

The format

Every card follows the same rules, so the feed reads like a deck you can flip through:

  • One question per card. Big, centred type and nothing else competing with it, so it works as a thumbnail in the grid.
  • Italics for proper names. Côte de Beaune, Gruet, and Saar are set apart from the question around them, the way a textbook would.
  • A running number and the handle. Each card carries @wine.study and its number, so reposts and screenshots still point back to the account. The public feed reaches card #249.
  • A question for followers in the caption. “Name your favorite producer from this region!” “How many of our followers have actually made wine before?” The cards started conversations, not just quizzes.

In early 2019 I refreshed the template with a faint wine-bottle outline behind the text, in muted red and green. It gives the cards a recognisable look without making them harder to read.

Getting it right

A study tool is only useful if it’s correct. Cards were written from cited sources, and when one went out asking the wrong question, I posted a public correction so nobody studied the mistake.

Results

The account grew to 4,400+ followers. Its bio linked to the Wine Study Flashcards platform at flashcards.wine, and story highlights collected WSET book picks, vintages, winemaking, and a Madeira quiz.
